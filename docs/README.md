# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central guide to OctoAcme’s project management processes, roles, and practices. OctoAcme manages cross-functional work through a lifecycle: initiate and validate an idea, plan scope and milestones, execute and track delivery, release safely, then capture lessons and improve.

## Process summary

- **Initiiation:** Validate the business need and measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, outline milestones and risks, and make a go/no-go decision before planning.
- **Planning:** Turn approved work into prioritized, estimated backlog items with acceptance criteria; define the Definition of Done, dependencies, risks, and release milestones.
- **Execution and tracking:** Deliver in small increments using the project board and pull request workflow. Monitor progress, quality, metrics, and blockers; escalate blockers through the team, PM, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate.
- **Risk and communication:** Maintain and review the risk register, coordinate dependencies, and share regular stakeholder updates through a single source of truth.
- **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans; deploy and verify, then communicate the release.
- **Retrospective and continuous improvement:** After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, capture what went well and what can improve, prioritize action items, and track their impact.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, schedules, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.
