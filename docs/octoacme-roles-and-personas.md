# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## UX / Product Designer

### Role Summary
The UX/Product Designer ensures that products meet user needs and provide a positive user experience.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Identify accessibility and usability requirements

### Goals
- Improve user satisfaction and accessibility
- Ensure solutions address real customer needs

### Typical Communication
- Collaborate with Product Managers on user needs and priorities
- Work with Developers on design feasibility and implementation
- Partner with QA on usability and acceptance testing

---

## Tech Lead / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
The Tech Lead provides technical direction and supports the team in delivering reliable and maintainable software.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical architecture and design decisions
- Review implementation quality and engineering standards
- Identify technical risks and dependencies
- Support Developers through technical guidance and mentoring

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality and consistency
- Reduce technical debt and delivery risks

### Typical Communication
- Coordinate with Project Managers on dependencies and schedules
- Discuss technical trade-offs with Product Managers
- Collaborate with Developers through design and code reviews

---

## Quality Engineer / Test Lead

### Role Summary
The Quality Engineer leads the testing strategy and helps the team deliver software that meets quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define risk-based testing strategies
- Coordinate test coverage and validation activities
- Track defects and release-quality evidence
- Support the existing QA/Testing function

### Goals
- Reduce defects and improve release quality
- Ensure acceptance criteria are properly validated

### Typical Communication
- Collaborate with Developers on testing and defect resolution
- Align testing with Product Manager acceptance criteria
- Report quality risks and release readiness to Project Managers

---

## Security and Privacy Specialist

### Role Summary
The Security and Privacy Specialist helps identify and manage security, privacy, and compliance risks.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security and privacy risks
- Recommend appropriate security controls
- Review applicable compliance requirements
- Support security validation before releases

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities
- Protect sensitive information and customer data

### Typical Communication
- Advise Developers and Tech Leads on security controls
- Partner with QA on security testing
- Escalate significant risks to Project Managers and Product Managers

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data/Analytics Lead helps the team measure product performance and understand project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement and instrumentation requirements
- Establish reliable reporting and analytics practices
- Validate product performance and outcome metrics

### Goals
- Support evidence-based decisions
- Improve visibility into product and project outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Collaborate with Product Managers on success metrics
- Work with Developers on data collection and instrumentation
- Provide progress and outcome insights to Project Managers

---

## Operations / Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)

### Role Summary
The Operations/SRE role supports reliable deployments, system availability, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define monitoring and operational requirements
- Support deployment planning and incident readiness
- Identify reliability and availability risks
- Maintain operational documentation and procedures

### Goals
- Improve system reliability and availability
- Reduce deployment risks and operational incidents

### Typical Communication
- Collaborate with Developers and Tech Leads on operational requirements
- Partner with QA on release-readiness checks
- Coordinate deployment risks with Project Managers

---

## Customer Support / Success Representative

### Role Summary
The Customer Support/Success Representative brings customer feedback and support requirements into project planning and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Collect and communicate customer feedback
- Identify customer-impacting issues
- Prepare support teams for upcoming releases
- Assist with customer-facing documentation

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Ensure support readiness for new releases

### Typical Communication
- Share customer feedback with Product Managers
- Coordinate support preparation with Project Managers
- Collaborate with Developers and QA on customer-reported issues

---

## Role Engagement and Decision Boundaries

| Role | When to Engage | Expected Outputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UX/Product Designer | Discovery, planning, and validation | Designs and usability findings |
| Tech Lead | Planning, design, and implementation | Technical decisions and risk assessments |
| Quality Engineer | Planning, development, and release | Test strategy and quality evidence |
| Security and Privacy Specialist | Planning, design, and release | Security recommendations and risk assessments |
| Data/Analytics Lead | Planning and outcome evaluation | Measurement plans and analytics reports |
| Operations/SRE | Design, deployment, and production support | Operational readiness and monitoring plans |
| Customer Support Representative | Planning, release, and post-release | Customer feedback and support-readiness guidance |

These personas complement the existing Developers, Product Managers,
Project Managers, QA/Testing teams, and Stakeholders.

Responsibilities and decision authority should be agreed upon during
project planning. Significant risks and unresolved decisions should
be escalated to the appropriate project or product leadership.

These are candidate roles, not mandatory separate positions.
Depending on project size and complexity, one person may perform
multiple roles. Role assignments should be reviewed with stakeholders
and adapted to project needs.
